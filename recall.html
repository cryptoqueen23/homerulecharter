Elections and recall

How city officials are elected, and how the charter provides for removing one before the end of a term.

What recall is

Recall is a process where residents collect signatures to force an election on whether an elected official should be removed from office before their term ends. This page does not encourage or oppose recalling any particular official. It explains what the charter provides.

The recall section has not been obtained

Who can be recalled, what grounds the charter requires, the signature requirement, the time restrictions, the petition process, the election process, and what happens if a recall succeeds are all set by the charter. That section has not been located yet, so none of it is stated here. A wrong signature number would send a resident down a dead end, which is worse than no number.
