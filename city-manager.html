Who controls the City Manager?

The city manager is appointed, not elected. This page shows the chain of authority and what is known about how that office is appointed, supervised and removed.

The City Manager is not elected. The City Manager serves under authority established by the Charter. Residents act on that office indirectly, by electing the council that appoints and supervises it.

Has the City Manager's authority changed?

Three measures on the November 2021 ballot touched this office: Measure H on council-approved absences from meetings, Measure K moving the personnel plan to an annual cycle, and Measure M applying the state conflict of interest standard. The charter sections governing appointment, evaluation and removal have not been obtained, so no comparison can be shown yet.
