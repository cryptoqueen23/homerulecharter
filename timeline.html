Charter timeline

Every known Copperas Cove charter election in order, with what was on the ballot and what evidence exists for it.

Why the timeline starts with a gap

The original adoption of the home rule charter is the first entry and it is unverified. A 1979 date circulates informally. It is not printed here as fact because the certified charter filed with the Texas Secretary of State has not been located. Filling that in with an assumption would undermine everything built on top of it.
