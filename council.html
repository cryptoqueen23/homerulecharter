What can the City Council do?

Budget, taxes, debt, ordinances, appointments, city manager oversight, elections and vacancies.

The one thing council cannot change alone

Council pay is set in the charter, which means it can only change if residents approve it at an election. Residents rejected an increase in November 2021, then rejected a larger one in November 2023 by a reported 1,393 against to 960 for. That is the clearest documented example on this site of a power that sits with residents rather than with the council.
