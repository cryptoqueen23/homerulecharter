What power do residents have?

Recall, initiative, referendum, charter amendments and city elections, each answered from the record where the record allows it.

Powers that come from state law, not the charter

Public Information Requests

Residents may request most city records under the Texas Public Information Act. This right comes from state law and does not depend on the city charter.

Open Meetings

The Texas Open Meetings Act requires most city business to happen in meetings open to the public with posted notice. The charter can add local requirements on top of that floor. Whether Copperas Cove does has not been confirmed.

Public Hearings

Certain decisions, including budget and tax rate actions, require a public hearing under state law. The charter may set additional hearings. Section not yet obtained.

Bond Elections

When the city calls a bond election, residents vote on the proposition. Not all city borrowing requires an election. See the Debt and Bonds page.
